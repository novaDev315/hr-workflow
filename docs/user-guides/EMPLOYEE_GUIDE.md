## Getting Started

### Downloading the App

**For Android**:
1. Open Google Play Store
2. Search for "HRFlow Pro"
3. Tap "Install"
4. Wait for installation to complete

**For iOS**:
1. Open App Store
2. Search for "HRFlow Pro"
3. Tap "Get"
4. Authenticate with Face ID/Touch ID
5. Wait for installation to complete

### First-Time Login

1. Open the HRFlow Pro app
2. You will see the login screen
3. Enter your credentials:
   - **Email**: Your company email address
   - **Password**: Provided by HR (you'll be prompted to change on first login)
4. Tap **"Sign In"**

### Changing Your Password (First Time)

1. After first login, you'll be prompted to change password
2. Enter:
   - Current password (temporary password from HR)
   - New password (must be strong: 8+ characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, special character)

3. Tap **"Update Password"**
4. You'll be taken to the home screen

### Enabling Permissions

For the app to work properly, you need to enable:

**Location Permission** (Required):
- Used for GPS-based clock in/out
- Ensures you're at the correct location
- When prompted, tap **"Allow While Using App"** or **"Always Allow"**

**Notifications** (Recommended):
- Receive updates about leave approvals
- Get payslip notifications
- Receive important announcements
- When prompted, tap **"Allow"**

**Camera** (Optional):
- Upload profile picture
- Scan documents if needed

## Clocking In/Out

### Understanding GPS Attendance

HRFlow Pro uses GPS to verify your location:
- You must be within **100 meters** of your work location
- The app will show your distance from the location
- Clock in/out is only enabled when you're within the geofence

### Clocking In

1. **Arrive at Your Work Location**:
   - Make sure you're physically at the office or assigned location
   - Ensure GPS is enabled on your phone

2. **Open the App**:
   - Tap on **"Attendance"** tab (clock icon)
   - Or use the **"Clock In"** button on home screen

3. **Wait for GPS Lock**:
   - App will automatically detect your location
   - You'll see:
     - Your current coordinates
     - Distance from work location
     - "Within geofence" message (green) or "Outside geofence" (red)

4. **Clock In**:
   - If within geofence, the **"Clock In"** button will be enabled (green)
   - Tap **"Clock In"**
   - You'll see a confirmation message
   - Your clock-in time is recorded

5. **What Gets Recorded**:
   - Exact clock-in time
   - Your GPS coordinates
   - Distance from location
   - Your device information

### Clocking Out

1. **At End of Work Day**:
   - Open the app
   - Go to **"Attendance"** tab

2. **Verify Location**:
   - Ensure you're still within geofence
   - If outside, move closer to the location

3. **Clock Out**:
   - Tap **"Clock Out"** button (blue)
   - Confirm action
   - System will calculate:
     - Total hours worked
     - Regular hours (up to 8 hours)
     - Overtime hours (if any)

4. **View Summary**:
   - After clocking out, you'll see:
     - Clock-in time
     - Clock-out time
     - Total duration
     - Overtime (if applicable)
     - Any late arrival/early departure flags

### What If...?

**I'm outside the geofence?**
- The clock in/out button will be disabled
- You'll see how far you are from the location
- Move closer (within 100m) to enable the button

**I forgot to clock in?**
- You can request an attendance correction
- Go to Attendance → Request Correction
- Select date and provide reason
- Your supervisor will review and approve/reject

**I forgot to clock out?**
- Submit a correction request
- Specify the actual clock-out time
- Provide a reason
- Wait for supervisor approval

**GPS is not working?**
- Ensure Location Services are enabled in phone settings
- Try closing and reopening the app
- Move to an area with better GPS signal (near window, outside)
- Contact your supervisor if issue persists

**I clock in late?**
- The system will mark it as late arrival
- Your supervisor will see the late arrival time
- It may affect attendance record
- Habitual late arrivals may require explanation

## Requesting Leave

### Types of Leave

Your organization supports various leave types:
- **Annual Leave**: Yearly vacation days
- **Sick Leave**: For illness
- **Casual Leave**: Short-term personal matters
- **Maternity/Paternity Leave**: For new parents
- **Unpaid Leave**: Without pay (if balance exhausted)

### Applying for Leave

1. **Go to Leave Screen**:
   - Tap **"Leave"** tab in bottom navigation

2. **Check Your Balance**:
   - At the top, you'll see your leave balance by type
   - Example: "Annual: 12 days | Sick: 5 days"

3. **Request New Leave**:
   - Tap **"Request Leave"** button (+) icon

4. **Fill Leave Form**:
   - **Leave Type** *: Select from dropdown (Annual, Sick, etc.)
   - **From Date** *: Start date of leave
   - **To Date** *: End date of leave
   - System automatically calculates number of days
   - **Reason** *: Explain why you need leave (be brief but clear)
   - **Emergency Contact** (optional): Phone number where you can be reached

5. **Review and Submit**:
   - Review all details
   - Check that your balance is sufficient
   - Tap **"Submit Request"**
   - You'll see confirmation message

6. **Track Status**:
   - Request will appear in your leave list with status:
     - **Pending L1**: Awaiting supervisor approval
     - **Pending L2**: Awaiting manager approval
     - **Pending L3**: Awaiting final approval
     - **Approved**: Leave approved
     - **Rejected**: Leave rejected (see reason)

### Leave Approval Process

Your leave request goes through multiple approvals:

```
Your Request
    ↓
Supervisor (Level 1)
    ↓
Manager (Level 2)
    ↓
Department Head (Level 3)
    ↓
APPROVED ✓
```

### Cancelling a Leave Request

If you need to cancel:

1. Go to **"Leave"** tab
2. Find your request (must be in Pending status)
3. Tap on the request
4. Tap **"Cancel Request"** button
5. Confirm cancellation
6. Note: Cannot cancel already approved leave; contact HR

### Viewing Leave History

1. In **"Leave"** tab, scroll down
2. See all past leave requests with:
   - Dates
   - Type
   - Status
   - Approver comments (if any)
3. Tap any request to see full details

## Viewing Payslips

### Accessing Payslips

1. Tap **"Payslips"** tab in bottom navigation
2. You'll see list of payslips by month:
   - Month and Year
   - Gross Salary
   - Net Salary
   - Status (Paid/Processing)

### Viewing Payslip Details

1. Tap on any payslip to open
2. You'll see detailed breakdown:

   **Earnings**:
   - Basic Salary
   - HRA (House Rent Allowance)
   - Conveyance Allowance
   - Medical Allowance
   - Special Allowance
   - Other Allowances
   - **Total Earnings (Gross Salary)**

   **Deductions**:
   - PF (Provident Fund)
   - ESI (Employee State Insurance)
   - Professional Tax
   - Income Tax (TDS)
   - Loan EMI (if any)
   - Other Deductions
   - **Total Deductions**

   **Net Payable**: Gross Salary - Total Deductions

   **Attendance Summary**:
   - Total Working Days
   - Days Present
   - Days Absent
   - Paid Leave Taken
   - Unpaid Leave (if any)

### Downloading Payslip

1. While viewing a payslip, tap the **Download** icon (⬇)
2. PDF will be downloaded to your device
3. You can share it via email or other apps
4. The payslip is password-protected (password: your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format)

### Understanding Your Payslip

**Gross Salary**: Total of all earnings before deductions

**Net Salary**: Take-home pay (Gross - Deductions)

**PF Contribution**:
- Employee contribution: 12% of basic
- Employer contribution: 12% of basic (not shown in deduction)

**Income Tax (TDS)**: Tax deducted at source based on your salary slab and declarations

### Questions About Payslip?

If you notice any discrepancy:
1. Check your attendance records first
2. Review any leave taken (unpaid leave reduces salary)
3. Check for any loan EMI deductions
4. If still unclear, contact HR with specific questions

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Cannot Log In

**Check**:
- Correct email and password
- Internet connection
- Caps Lock is not on
- Password hasn't expired

**Solution**:
- Tap "Forgot Password" to reset
- Contact HR if account is locked

#### GPS Not Working / Cannot Clock In

**Check**:
- Location Services are enabled (Phone Settings → Privacy → Location)
- HRFlow app has location permission
- You're within 100m of work location
- GPS signal is strong (try moving outdoors)

**Solution**:
- Restart the app
- Toggle airplane mode on/off
- Restart your phone
- If persists, contact supervisor

#### App Keeps Crashing

**Solutions**:
- Clear app cache: Phone Settings → Apps → HRFlow → Clear Cache
- Update to latest version from Play Store/App Store
- Uninstall and reinstall the app
- Ensure phone OS is up to date

#### Not Receiving Notifications

**Check**:
- Notifications are enabled for HRFlow (Phone Settings → Apps → HRFlow → Notifications)
- Phone is not in Do Not Disturb mode
- Internet connection is active

**Solution**:
- Re-enable notifications in settings
- Log out and log back in

#### Payslip Not Loading

**Solutions**:
- Check internet connection
- Pull down to refresh
- Clear app cache
- If specific month is missing, it might not be processed yet by HR

## FAQ

### General

**Q: Can I use the app from anywhere?**
A: You can view information from anywhere, but clock in/out only works when you're at your assigned work location (within 100m).

**Q: Does the app work offline?**
A: Limited functionality offline. You can view cached data, but clock in/out, leave requests, and real-time data require internet.

**Q: How secure is my data?**
A: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your credentials are securely stored. The app uses industry-standard security practices.

### Attendance

**Q: What if I work at multiple locations?**
A: Your supervisor will assign you to multiple locations. The app will show the nearest location when you clock in. You can also manually select the location.

**Q: Can I clock in for a colleague?**
A: No. GPS verification ensures each person clocks in from the correct location. Proxy attendance is not allowed.

**Q: What happens if I'm late?**
A: The system records late arrival time. Your supervisor will see it during approval. Frequent late arrivals may be flagged.

**Q: How is overtime calculated?**
A: Any hours worked beyond 8 hours in a day are counted as overtime. This appears in your attendance record and affects salary.

### Leave

**Q: How far in advance should I apply for leave?**
A: Apply at least 3-5 days in advance for planned leave. For sick leave, apply as soon as possible.

**Q: Can I take leave during my notice period?**
A: Generally no, unless approved by HR. Check your company policy.

**Q: What happens if I run out of leave balance?**
A: You can still apply, but it will be marked as unpaid leave and will affect your salary.

**Q: Can I check my team members' leave?**
A: If you're a supervisor, yes. Regular employees can only see their own leave.

### Payslips

**Q: When are payslips available?**
A: Typically by the 5th of each month for the previous month. Your HR will announce the exact date.

**Q: Why is my salary less this month?**
A: Check for:
- Unpaid leave taken
- Loan EMI deductions
- Additional tax deductions
- Late/absent days
Review your payslip details or contact HR.

**Q: Can I download all my payslips at once?**
A: Currently, you need to download each payslip individually. Bulk download feature is coming soon.
